About the data

Eviction filings come from the NY State Unified Court System's deidentified landlord-tenant extract. In New York City it covers the Civil Court in all five boroughs plus the Harlem and Red Hook community justice centers, so NYC counts are complete. Outside NYC it covers the 61 city courts and the Nassau and Suffolk district courts. Town and village courts are not in the extract, so most suburban and rural ZIP codes appear as no data, not as places without evictions, and ZIP codes that straddle a city line are partly undercounted. For the same reason, totals outside NYC are a floor, not a full count. Commercial cases are excluded. A filing is counted in the month it was filed and in the ZIP code of the property.

Coverage. Every NYC ZIP code is covered. Outside NYC, a ZIP code is shown when its courts in the extract recorded at least 100 residential filings over the period. Each ZIP code is assigned to NYC or outside NYC by where most of its cases were filed.

Rates are annualized: filings in the selected window, divided by the number of months, times 12, per 1,000 renter households. Tiers split covered ZIP codes statewide into quartiles of the January 2022 to latest-month rate, so a color means the same rate in every area and window. Area rates in the comparison table use renter households in covered ZIP codes only.

Renter profile figures are ACS 5-year estimates for ZIP Code Tabulation Areas. Income needed to afford rent is median gross rent × 12 ÷ 0.30. Race and ethnicity describe the householder of renter-occupied units; Hispanic householders may be of any race, and "White" means White alone, not Hispanic.
